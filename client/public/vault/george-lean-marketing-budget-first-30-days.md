# George: Lean Marketing Budget — First 30 Days

**90-day lean budget:** R18,000  
**Recommended first-30-day allocation:** **R8,000**  
**Budget retained for Days 31–90:** **R10,000**  
**Market assumption:** South Africa  
**Operating assumption:** Education-led enquiry until George’s final operating model and advertising eligibility are confirmed

## Recommended allocation

| Budget category | Days 1–30 allocation | Share of Month 1 | Purpose and expected output |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Compliance and professional review | R1,500 | 18.75% | Confirm the operating model, review initial claims and disclaimers, identify required registrations or professional controls, and approve the response boundaries. |
| Website and landing-page work | R2,000 | 25.00% | Improve or build one mobile-friendly landing page, enquiry form, privacy wording, CTA, thank-you state, WhatsApp routing, and essential trust sections. |
| Analytics, CRM, and tracking | R750 | 9.38% | Configure GA4, Search Console, UTMs, conversion events, and a simple lead log recording source, status, outcome, and permission. |
| SEO and content production | R1,500 | 18.75% | Prepare the first core article or model guide, one FAQ page, basic on-page optimization, and the first two weeks of approved copy. |
| Organic social production | R1,000 | 12.50% | Produce the first six calendar items: two short videos, two carousels, and two FAQ/website assets. Use founder-led recording and lightweight editing. |
| Email/WhatsApp software | R250 | 3.13% | Support enquiry confirmation, approved response templates, consent records, and limited permission-based follow-up. |
| Contingency reserve | R1,000 | 12.50% | Hold for compliance corrections, form or tracking fixes, design resizing, hosting or plugin needs, or an urgent copy revision. |
| Google Search media | R0 | 0.00% | No paid-search release during the first 30 days. Eligibility, tracking, landing-page alignment, response capacity, and economics must be confirmed first. |
| **Total** | **R8,000** | **100.00%** | Month 1 creates a trustworthy and measurable foundation rather than buying traffic prematurely. |

## What the first R8,000 must deliver

By Day 30, George should have one documented operating model, one approved conversion action, a functioning landing page, a tested enquiry process, basic privacy and consent controls, a claim register, source and lead tracking, one core educational article or guide, the first six social assets, approved comment and enquiry scripts, and a simple KPI baseline.

The first month is successful when the business can answer these questions with evidence: **Who is the offer for? What model is being marketed? What can be claimed? Where does the visitor go? Who responds? What data is collected? Can the source and outcome of every enquiry be tracked?**

## Costs not included in the R8,000

The allocation excludes stock, fulfilment, delivery, payment fees, product photography beyond basic founder-led production, a full website rebuild, ongoing employee salaries, medical or pharmacy services, registration or licensing fees, laboratory testing, and major legal work. If any of these are required before marketing can begin, they must be budgeted separately or funded from an explicitly approved reallocation.

## Procurement rules for the lean scenario

George should obtain a defined deliverable before releasing each supplier payment. Website work should be priced against a single landing-page scope rather than an open-ended redesign. Content should be batched from the six approved Week 1–2 templates. Software should start on free or entry-level plans where those plans meet privacy and operational requirements. The contingency should remain uncommitted until a specific issue is documented.

No part of the first-month budget should be moved into Google, Meta, TikTok, influencer, or boosted-post spend simply because foundation work finishes under budget. Paid activity requires a separate release decision after the operating model, claims, destination, tracking, response capacity, platform eligibility, and customer-acquisition ceiling are confirmed.

## Day-30 release gate for the remaining R10,000

The remaining lean budget should be released only if the following conditions are satisfied:

| Gate | Minimum requirement |
|---|---|
| Operating model | One model is selected and consistently reflected in copy, CTA, destination, and response flow. |
| Claims | All material claims have a source, scope, reviewer, and approval status. |
| Conversion path | Mobile and desktop enquiries work, notifications arrive, and the thank-you state is clear. |
| Tracking | Source, enquiry, qualified status, outcome, consent, and spend can be recorded. |
| Response capacity | A named owner can meet the stated response standard. |
| Supply or service capacity | Stock, fulfilment, or consultation capacity can support the planned demand. |
| Economics | Gross profit, fulfilment cost, payment cost, desired contribution, and affordable acquisition ceiling are entered. |
| Platform eligibility | Any paid channel is independently confirmed as eligible for the exact business, offer, country, account, domain, claims, and landing page. |

## Recommended management decision

Approve **R7,000 for planned Month 1 work** and keep **R1,000 as controlled contingency**. Do not release paid-media funds during the first 30 days. At the Day-30 review, reconcile actual spend and assess whether the remaining R10,000 should fund a compliant Google Search test, deeper organic search content, referral outreach, conversion improvements, or further compliance and technical work.

> This is a planning allocation, not legal, regulatory, medical, or financial advice. Product status, advertising eligibility, claims, privacy controls, supplier pricing, capacity, and unit economics require appropriate verification before implementation.
